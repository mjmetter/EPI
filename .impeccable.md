## Design Context

### Users
Mixed audience sharing one artifact: engineering teams reviewing their own delivery trends in a monthly retro, and engineering leadership looking across teams for where to invest in improvement. The report must work at both scan-speed (immediate signal: band and direction per metric) and deep-read (metric breakdowns, repo comparisons, AI adoption signals). It describes teams and systems, never individuals.

### Brand Personality
Calm, dense, precise. Trustworthy rather than judgmental — this is an instrument for spotting bottlenecks and starting conversations, not a performance scorecard. The report should feel carefully made and credible, not like a generic dashboard generator.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Precision instrument.** Think Bloomberg terminal, aviation instrument panel, or a financial briefing document. Dense but structured. Every pixel earns its place. No decoration.

**Theme**: Dark. Team retros and monthly reviews — often projected on a shared screen.

**Color palette**: Deep charcoal (warm near-black) + amber/gold accent. NOT the current navy + indigo which is the AI-design monoculture. The amber accent reads as premium and decisive — Bloomberg energy. Traffic-light band colors (green/amber/red) are functional, retained but refined via OKLCH.

**Typography**:
- UI text + labels: **Barlow Condensed** (600, 700) — condensed grotesque, authority through compression, maximum information density
- Body + table text: **Barlow** (400, 500) — same family, opens up for readability
- Numeric values + scores: **Azeret Mono** — tabular monospace with a distinctive technical character, not the usual IBM/JetBrains

**Layout**: No rounded hero cards. No gradient backgrounds on big numbers. No box shadows. Sharp 1px borders. Dense tabular grids. Structure communicates clarity — the data does the work.

### Design Principles
1. **Signal before decoration** — each metric's band and direction should be readable in 3 seconds. Remove anything that competes with that.
2. **Density earns trust** — engineers trust data-dense reports that show their workings more than spacious "designed" dashboards. Compression is a feature.
3. **Functional color only** — amber accent for interactive/current-period highlights; green/amber/red strictly for band encoding. No decorative use of color.
4. **Typography does the work** — hierarchy through weight and condensed width, not through rounded containers and gradients.
5. **Consistent structure** — tabular grids and consistent column alignment over heterogeneous card arrangements.

### Anti-references
- The current dark navy (#0f172a) + indigo (#818cf8) + rounded-corner cards
- Glowing "all-elite shimmer" animations
- Gradient-filled hero metric numbers
- Generic admin SaaS dashboards (Grafana, Datadog aesthetic)
